## 1. Polymer Family Selection Matrix

Different hot melt adhesive film chemistries are not interchangeable. Match the polymer family to substrate polarity, elasticity, wash durability, and thermal limits before running factory sample trials.

| Polymer Family | Core Material Characteristics | Typical Industrial Applications | Reference Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TPU** (Thermoplastic Polyurethane) | High elasticity, soft hand feel, strong wash durability, flexible bonding to fabrics, TPU, leather, and foam | Seamless underwear & activewear, no-sew shoe uppers, waterproof garments, bags, embroidery patches | [How to Choose TPU Hot Melt Adhesive Film](https://tpuhotmeltadhesivefilm.com/how-to-choose-tpu-hot-melt-adhesive-film/) |
| **PES** (Copolyester) | High bond strength on polyester, metals, and rigid substrates; strong dry-cleaning and heat resistance | Luggage lamination, automotive headliners, emblems, industrial composites | [What Is Hot Melt Adhesive Film?](https://tpuhotmeltadhesivefilm.com/what-is-hot-melt-adhesive-film/) |
| **PA** (Polyamide) | Good dry-cleaning resistance and crisp adhesion to textiles and leather | Apparel interlining, leather goods, embroidery backing | [Embroidery Patch Backing Guide](https://tpuhotmeltadhesivefilm.com/tpu-hot-melt-adhesive-film-for-embroidery-patches/) |
| **PO** (Polyolefin) | Formulated for low-surface-energy plastics (PP, PE), foams, and general composites | Automotive interior trim, acoustic foam bonding, industrial laminates | [Adhesive Film Lamination Guide](https://tpuhotmeltadhesivefilm.com/adhesive-film-lamination-hot-melt-film-bonds-fabric/) |
| **EVA** (Ethylene-Vinyl Acetate) | Lower activation temperature window for heat-sensitive substrates | Paper, wood veneer, light foam, low-temperature composites | [Adhesive Film Lamination Guide](https://tpuhotmeltadhesivefilm.com/adhesive-film-lamination-hot-melt-film-bonds-fabric/) |

## 2. Common Lamination Defects & Troubleshooting Checklist

| Observed Defect | Likely Root Causes | Factory Verification Step | Detailed Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Post-wash delamination** | DWR/silicone finish on fabric, insufficient glue-line melt temperature, short dwell time, testing before full cooling | Measure actual glue-line temperature; run peel test after 24h cure + wash cycles | [Why Laminated Fabric Delaminates](https://tpuhotmeltadhesivefilm.com/why-laminated-fabric-delaminates/) |
| **Bubbling / air pockets** | Trapped fabric moisture, uneven platen pressure, excessive heat volatilizing substrate coatings | Pre-dry hygroscopic fabrics; verify press pressure uniformity | [Why Hot Melt Film Bubbles](https://tpuhotmeltadhesivefilm.com/why-hot-melt-adhesive-film-bubbles-during-lamination/) |
| **Adhesive strike-through** | Film too thick for open-weave/thin knit, excessive press temperature or pressure | Step down film thickness or lower temperature/pressure incrementally | [Seamless Apparel TPU Bonding](https://tpuhotmeltadhesivefilm.com/tpu-hot-melt-adhesive-film-seamless-underwear/) |
| **Weak edge bonding on shoe uppers** | Uneven curvature pressure or scorch-limited temperature on synthetic leather | Verify lower-temp TPU grade + peel & flex fatigue testing | [Footwear Shoe Upper Bonding](https://tpuhotmeltadhesivefilm.com/tpu-hot-melt-adhesive-film-shoe-upper-bonding/) |

## 3. Release Liner Comparison

- **Release Paper**: Suited to flat-press bonding, die-cut patches, and manual positioning.
- **PET Release Film**: Smooth surface finish and high dimensional stability for precision roll-to-roll converting.
- **Liner-Free Film**: Eliminates liner waste in continuous direct-lamination lines (by grade).

Full comparison: [Release Paper vs. PET Release Film for Hot Melt Adhesive Film](https://tpuhotmeltadhesivefilm.com/release-paper-vs-pet-release-film-hot-melt-adhesive-film/)
